## Store listing (English)

**Product name:** Taskbar Monitor

**Short description:**

```
CPU, GPU, VRAM and RAM usage right on the taskbar. Click for details.
```

**Description:**

```
Taskbar Monitor keeps an eye on your PC's load without opening Task Manager. A small bar on the taskbar shows CPU, GPU and RAM usage, updated every second, with the same numbers Task Manager reports.

Click the bar for a detail panel:
• CPU: load per thread, clock speed and a 60-second history graph
• RAM: in use, available and committed memory
• GPU: 3D, copy and video load for every graphics card (NVIDIA, AMD and Intel, including laptops with two GPUs), VRAM and shared memory
• System: processes, threads, handles and up time

Right-click the bar to choose which metrics to show (CPU, GPU, VRAM, RAM), put the bar on the left or right of the taskbar, start with Windows, or switch between English and Vietnamese.

Light and out of the way:
• A small native app (under 1 MB) that uses well under 1% of one CPU core
• Values turn yellow at 60% and red at 85%
• Hides automatically while a game or video is fullscreen
• No account, no ads, no data collection
```
